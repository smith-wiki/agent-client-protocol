# ACP is jointly governed through an interim, transparent maintainer hierarchy

ACP’s interim model is jointly governed by Zed and JetBrains, with an eventual move to an independent foundation as the stated direction. ([governance, L1–L3](https://agentclientprotocol.com/community/governance))

The hierarchy separates participation from authority:

- **Contributors** submit code, documentation, issues, and proposals, but have no formal decision power. ([governance, L26–L28](https://agentclientprotocol.com/community/governance))
- **Maintainers** independently run a component or Working/Interest Group, engage contributors, maintain its roadmap and documentation, and escalate when needed. ([governance, L29–L37](https://agentclientprotocol.com/community/governance))
- **Core maintainers** can write to the ACP repository, review and merge work, steer the specification, resolve disputes, and appoint or remove maintainers. ([governance, L39–L46](https://agentclientprotocol.com/community/governance))
- **Lead maintainers (BDFLs)** Ben Brandt of Zed and Sergey Ignatov of JetBrains may veto maintainer decisions and coordinate official matters, project direction, and expenses; they appoint core maintainers. ([governance, L48–L53](https://agentclientprotocol.com/community/governance)) ([governance, L105–L108](https://agentclientprotocol.com/community/governance))

Together, maintainers, core maintainers, and leads form the **ACP steering group**. ([governance, L10–L15](https://agentclientprotocol.com/community/governance)) Interest Groups surface and articulate problems; Working Groups produce concrete deliverables such as [RFDs](rfd-process.md) or community implementations. Both self-govern publicly through documented processes, GitHub issues/PRs, and an official Zulip channel. ([governance, L65–L81](https://agentclientprotocol.com/community/governance)) Core maintainers meet biweekly and publish proposal notes; anyone can raise an agenda topic in the public Zulip chat and may be invited to that otherwise private meeting. ([governance, L86–L94](https://agentclientprotocol.com/community/governance))
