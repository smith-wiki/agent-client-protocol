# Tool calls stream observable work, while permissions and elicitation keep consequential choices with the user.

In stable ACP v1, an Agent reports a new call through `session/update` as `tool_call`: `toolCallId` identifies it, `title` describes it, and `kind` guides Client presentation ([Tool Calls, lines 12–48](https://agentclientprotocol.com/protocol/v1/tool-calls)). Kinds are `read`, `edit`, `delete`, `move`, `search`, `execute`, `think`, `fetch`, `switch_mode`, and `other`; statuses are `pending` (the default: input still streaming or awaiting approval), `in_progress`, `completed`, and `failed` ([Tool Calls, lines 137–148](https://agentclientprotocol.com/protocol/v1/tool-calls); literals from [schema.json L465–L543](https://github.com/agentclientprotocol/agent-client-protocol/blob/b44babba0bbcd398fa36ee0545a6de9c147c67c0/schema/v1/schema.json#L465-L543)). Later `tool_call_update` notifications identify the call by `toolCallId` and include only changed fields ([Tool Calls, lines 64–70](https://agentclientprotocol.com/protocol/v1/tool-calls)).

Content items are tagged by `type`: `content` wraps an ordinary [content block](content-blocks.md), `diff` carries required `path` and `newText` plus nullable `oldText` (null for a new file), and `terminal` embeds live output by `terminalId`; `locations` hold a required absolute `path` and optional `line` ([Tool Calls, lines 151–201](https://agentclientprotocol.com/protocol/v1/tool-calls); [schema.json L545–L600, L1019–L1094](https://github.com/agentclientprotocol/agent-client-protocol/blob/b44babba0bbcd398fa36ee0545a6de9c147c67c0/schema/v1/schema.json#L545-L1094)). [Client filesystem and terminals](client-filesystem-and-terminals.md) explains the referenced capabilities.

Before execution, the Agent may call `session/request_permission` with `sessionId`, a `toolCall`, and labeled options: `allow_once`, `allow_always`, `reject_once`, or `reject_always` ([Tool Calls, lines 73–95](https://agentclientprotocol.com/protocol/v1/tool-calls); [lines 111–135](https://agentclientprotocol.com/protocol/v1/tool-calls)). Its outcome is `selected` with `optionId`, or `cancelled` when the [prompt turn](prompt-turn.md) is cancelled ([Tool Calls, lines 96–110](https://agentclientprotocol.com/protocol/v1/tool-calls)).

Structured input uses `elicitation/create`, scoped by `sessionId` (optionally `toolCallId`) or `requestId`, after support is advertised in `clientCapabilities.elicitation` during [initialization](initialization.md) ([Elicitation, lines 20–44](https://agentclientprotocol.com/protocol/v1/elicitation)). Form mode collects non-sensitive data; URL mode handles out-of-band workflows, and responses are `accept`, `decline`, or `cancel` ([Elicitation, lines 1–5](https://agentclientprotocol.com/protocol/v1/elicitation); [lines 71–81](https://agentclientprotocol.com/protocol/v1/elicitation)). The ACP page does not declare stability and explicitly derives from an MCP 2026-07-28 release-candidate specification, so that underlying model should not be presented as finalized ([Elicitation, line 1](https://agentclientprotocol.com/protocol/v1/elicitation)).
